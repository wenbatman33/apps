# Simple board-game house reference analysis

## Identification

- Target: small injection-moulded board-game house token; primary domain `object`; confidence 0.97.
- Intended use: real-time mobile Three.js property building, displayed in groups of one to three.

## Overall form and silhouette

- Geometric, bilaterally symmetric compound solid.
- Main bounding volume is a short rectangular cuboid with a compact gable prism above it.
- Roof is wider than the wall body and creates a clearly visible overhang on all four sides.
- The front and rear wall end in triangular gables under the ridge.

## Macro, meso, and micro hierarchy

- Macro: one-piece house token.
- Meso: wall body, gable roof, roof ridge/vent bump, shallow base lip.
- Micro: softened injection-moulded edges, subtle roof-tile relief, door/window relief kept broad enough to read at game scale.

## Spatial relationships

- Roof sits flush on the wall top with an overlapping overhang.
- Ridge runs along the long roof axis.
- Base lip is attached below the wall body and slightly exceeds its footprint.
- Door and window relief are embedded shallowly into the visible wall faces.

## Material and finish

- Opaque coloured plastic, metalness 0, medium-low roughness with a broad specular highlight.
- The owner/region colour applies to the complete token rather than only decorative trim.
- Bevels must be real geometry so the small object remains readable under the board key light.

## Identity-defining features

- Very simple Monopoly-like house-token silhouette.
- Compact proportions; no pagoda, curved eaves, columns, gold ornament, separate roof rolls, or realistic architectural detailing.
- Thick gable roof overhang and single-colour injection-moulded appearance.

## Uncertainty and limits

- Reference is a single low-resolution photograph; rear and underside are hidden.
- Door/window relief is not consistently visible across the coloured examples, so it will be stylised and shallow.
- Exact manufacturing dimensions cannot be inferred; proportions are reconstructed for mobile gameplay readability.

## Suitability verdict

- Pass for a stylised procedural game prop. The silhouette and material family are clear, and hidden sides are safely inferred by symmetry.
