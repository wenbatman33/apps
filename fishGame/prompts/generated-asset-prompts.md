# Generated Asset Prompts

These prompts were used to create the first Phaser 3 portrait fishing arcade asset pack.

## Loading Screen

Premium 1080x1920 mobile portrait loading screen for a fishing arcade game. Luminous deep ocean temple, turquoise and sapphire water, sun rays, coral reef silhouettes, bubbles, ancient pearl gate, heroic golden cannon silhouette, colorful fish, lower area left quiet for a loading bar. No text, logos, or watermark.

## Main Menu

Premium 1080x1920 mobile portrait main menu background. Bright tropical underwater palace entrance, corals, pearls, treasure coins, clean blue water, sunlight caustics, empty golden title plaque, ornate cannon focal point, blank shell-shaped button frames. No text, logos, or watermark.

## Gameplay Background

Premium 1080x1920 mobile portrait gameplay arena, strict top-down view. Looking straight down at luminous shallow ocean floor, coral reefs, rocks, sea plants, pearl fragments, sand ripples, subtle ancient ruin tiles around edges, quiet center for gameplay. No fish, cannon, UI, text, logos, or watermark.

## Fish Sprite Sheet

Transparent-ready 4x3 sprite sheet on flat chroma-key background. Twelve distinct polished 2.5D arcade fish and bonus creatures in strict top-down dorsal view, all facing right for runtime rotation: clownfish, yellow tang, blue striped fish, purple round fish, pufferfish, manta ray, small shark, golden prize fish, jellyfish bonus, anglerfish mini-boss, crab target, pearl turtle bonus. No text or labels.

## Cannon And Effects Sprite Sheet

Transparent-ready 4x3 sprite sheet on flat chroma-key background. Four golden cannon upgrade levels, pearl bullets, energy harpoons, expanding fishing nets, coin burst, and splash impact. No text or labels.

## UI Sprite Sheet

Transparent-ready 4x4 sprite sheet on flat chroma-key background. Blank gold-blue HUD bar, counters, blank buttons, common UI icons, progress bar frame, badge, and close button. No words, letters, or numbers.
